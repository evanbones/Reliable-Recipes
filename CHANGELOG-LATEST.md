### Added

- JEI integration on all versions:
    - Custom anvil repair materials now show up in JEI, along with custom brewing recipes and replaced brewing ingredients (up to 26.2).
    - Recipes removed by rules, or that use hidden items, are now hidden from JEI.
    - In dev mode, pressing Delete while hovering a recipe in JEI's recipe screen deletes it, and `/rrecipes_undo` brings it back right away.
